System Architecture

Built on a Rock-Solid Data Foundation

DairySync's database is fully normalised and designed around 10 core entities — ensuring zero data redundancy and maximum performance.

🏡 Farm
🐄 Animal
🧬 Breed
💉 Vaccination

🥛 MilkProduction
🥣 FeedConsumption
🌾 Feed + FeedCategory
📦 FeedStock

Ownership Rule

Each Farmer can own one or more Farms, but every Farm should belongs to atleast one Farmer — ensuring clear accountability.

Farm–Animal Rule

A Farm can house multiple Animals, but each Animal is maintained at one specific Farm at a time — no ambiguous locations.

Breed Classification

Every Animal must be classified under exactly one Breed, enabling consistent performance benchmarking across your herd.

Health History

An Animal can undergo multiple VeterinaryVisits and receive multiple Vaccinations — complete lifetime medical records, always accessible.

Milk Recording Rule

MilkProduction is tied to an individual animal and recorded twice daily. TotalMilk = MorningMilk + EveningMilk — automatically computed.

Feed Integrity Rule

Feed products must belong to a FeedCategory and maintain a FeedStock record. FeedConsumption links animal, feed, quantity, and date — no gaps.
